SPARK Home is the Executive Command Surface — not a feed, not a SaaS dashboard, not a shrunk desktop.
The executive opens SPARK and immediately sees: what needs a decision, what SPARK is doing, what opportunities exist — then acts once.
Match the onboard story cleanliness (quiet dark, one clear hierarchy, soft cards, restrained purple, no chrome noise) but make Home sharper and more operational than setup.

Block specs
1 — Header (onboard-grade quiet)

Greeting: Good morning, {name} — medium weight, not billboard
Super Spark pill: same family as live AIChatPill (compact, premium, right-aligned)
Status line: single line, 12–13px muted
Live green pulse when active
Copy pattern: Spark is active · {n} opportunities · {n} need review

No second toolbar, no date row unless microscopic and muted
No Spark logo inside a chat avatar

2 — Needs You (hero of the screen)
Label: Needs you (not “Priority Queue” / “Tasks”)
Each row = one decision card:

Soft left accent (warning / accent / success) — thinner and calmer than harsh bars
Title = human outcome (“2 reviews waiting”)
Subtitle = one concrete item title
Trailing: Review → / Open →
Empty state: one calm line — “You’re clear. SPARK is watching.” — not empty gray boxes

Max 3 visible decisions; overflow implied by navigation to Review / Viral Sparks.
3 — Pipeline (intelligence, not a project tool)

Horizontal 5 quiet nodes (Research → Produce → Review → Publish → Learn) or live stage labels from product
Current stage emphasized; others whisper
One micro-line under: what SPARK is doing now (optional)
No heavy progress bars, no % spam on Home

4 — Opportunities (Viral Sparks preview)

Section label: Opportunities
1–2 large cards only (thumbnail optional, title, spark signal, Open →)
If zero: hide section or one line “Research is warming up” — no fake sparks
Card style = onboard card language (radius, border, quiet fill)

5 — At a glance (metrics)

2×2 grid, large number, small label
Live mappings: Reviews pending · Viral Sparks · In production · Approved / Ready
Tap → correct route (/review, viral sparks, etc.)
No sparklines, no 8-tile dashboard

6 — Recent (optional, tertiary)

Short list (2–3), activity style
Or omit if Needs You + Opportunities already tell the story (Apple would remove)
